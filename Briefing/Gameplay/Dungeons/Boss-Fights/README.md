# Boss Fight Mechanics

Boss encounters across all race categories, including abilities, counter strategies, phase transitions, and guaranteed blueprint rewards. Cross-references: [Races](../../../factions/Races/README.md).

---

## Boss Types by Race Category

| Race Type | Boss Ability | Counter Strategy | Reward Tier |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Bugs (Swarm Queen) | Summons 4-8 minion bugs every 30 seconds | Area denial weapons, flame throwers | Advanced blueprints + rare minerals |
| Reptilians (Alpha Predator) | Camouflage for 15 seconds, ambush attack | Thermal imaging, sonic sensors | Weapon blueprints + armor upgrades |
| Dinos (Titan Rex) | Ground slam AoE (20m radius), charge attack | Kite and whittle, use cover | Hull parts + fuel reserves |
| Humans (Commander) | Calls reinforcements, deploys turret network | Priority target elimination | Full military blueprint pack |
| Avian (Sky Dominator) | Aerial assault, dive bombing, wind gusts | Anti-air weapons, EMP grenades | Flight module blueprints |
| Amorphous (Blob Entity) | Absorbs damage, splits on low HP | Continuous damage, fire/ice combo | Alien alloy + dark matter crystals |
| Aquatic (Leviathan) | Underwater movement, flood generation | Drain area first, then engage | Water turbine + hull reinforcement |
| Plant-like (Ancient Treant) | Root traps, spore clouds, regenerative field | Fire damage, sever roots | Survival boosters + crystal components |
| Mechanical (War Machine) | Laser beams, missile volleys, shield bubble | EMP weapons, exploit cooldown windows | Energy core + void modules |

## Boss Fight Phases

| Phase | Trigger Condition | Behavior Change | Player Strategy |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Phase 1 | Boss spawn | Standard attack patterns, 100% HP | Identify weak points, establish positioning |
| Phase 2 | HP drops below 70% | Second ability unlocked, +20% attack speed | Maintain distance, use cover |
| Phase 3 | HP drops below 40% | Enrage mode — all abilities active, summons minions | Focus fire, manage adds, use AoE |
| Phase 4 | HP drops below 15% | Final stand — damage output doubled, defense halved | All-out assault, deploy ultimate weapons |

## Boss Blueprint Guarantee

All bosses guarantee at least **1 blueprint** from the appropriate TIR tier:

| Boss Tier | Guaranteed Blueprints | Additional Roll Chance |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| TIR 1 boss | 1x Ring 1 blueprint | 25% chance for bonus basic materials |
| TIR 2 boss | 1x Ring 2 blueprint | 35% chance for bonus minerals/hull |
| TIR 3 boss | 1x Ring 3 blueprint | 45% chance for dark matter crystals |
| TIR 4 boss | 1x Outer Ring blueprint + 1x Ring 3 | 55% chance for advanced alloy parts |
| TIR 5 boss | 2x Outer Ring blueprints | 70% chance for any rare material |
