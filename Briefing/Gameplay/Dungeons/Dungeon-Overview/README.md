# Dungeon Overview — Entry Methods and Size Classification

Dungeons are instanced or semi-instanced areas containing structures, enemies, puzzles, and valuable loot. They serve as the primary source of technology blueprints for ship modules, weapons, armor upgrades, and building enhancements. They are also the main source of equippable gear — swappable weapons, armor, and utility items that keep early units relevant through late game [See Equipment System](../../../units/Equipment-System/README.md).

---

## Dungeon Entry Methods [See Navigation](../../../Planets/Navigation/README.md)

| Method | Requirement | Detection Range | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|-------|
| On-foot exploration | Basic radar array (TIR 1) | 200m radius | Reveals surface structures |
| Deep sonar scan | Energy Lab research (TIR 2) | 500m underground | Detects buried dungeons |
| Satellite monitoring | Dark Matter Lab (TIR 3) | Planet-wide | Tracks enemy movement patterns |
| Quantum scanner | Void Lab (TIR 5) | Instant full reveal | Complete map disclosure |

## Dungeon Size Classification

| Size Tier | Room Count | Estimated Clear Time | Enemy Density | Loot Quality |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|---------------|--------------|
| Tiny (Scavenged Vehicle) | 1-2 rooms | 5-10 minutes | Low | Basic materials, 30-80 hull parts |
| Small (Abandoned House) | 3-6 rooms | 10-20 minutes | Low-Moderate | Basic weapons, 50-200 minerals |
| Medium (Enemy Camp, Military Outpost) | 7-15 rooms + outdoor area | 20-45 minutes | Moderate-High | Blueprints, 200-800 minerals |
| Large (Hospital Complex, Alien Structure) | 16-30 rooms | 45-90 minutes | High | Advanced blueprints, 500-2000 minerals |
| Fortress (Inner Ring Citadel) | 30+ rooms + courtyard | 90-180 minutes | Very High | Elite blueprints, 1000-5000 minerals, boss rewards |
