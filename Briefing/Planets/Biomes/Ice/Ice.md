# Ice Biome

## Visual
Bright white ice sheets. Extreme Cold.

## Resources
- **Energy:** Wind+40%, Geothermal(high), Solar-30%
- **Fuel:** Frozen methane(abundant), Oil under ice
- **Construction:** Stone(rare), Ice blocks(unique)
- **Minerals:** Crystal(very abundant), Dark Matter(poles)
- **Survival:** Food-40% farm, Oxygen(std ice)

## Unique Effect
Ice cracking destroys unanchored buildings

## Hostile Area
Polar alien monolith. Boss: Ice Colossus.
