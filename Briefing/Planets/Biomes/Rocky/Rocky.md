# Rocky Biome

## Visual
Gray/brown exposed rock. Variable.

## Resources
- **Energy:** Geothermal, Wind+15%
- **Fuel:** Limited oil
- **Construction:** Stone(v.high), Metal Ore(abundant)
- **Minerals:** All std, Titanium(inc)
- **Survival:** Food-20%, Oxygen std

## Unique Effect
Rock formations +15% wall HP

## Hostile Area
Volcanic crater fortress. Boss: Magma Golem Lord.
