# Swamp Biome

## Visual
Brown-green murky. Warm.

## Resources
- **Energy:** Water Turbines(reduced), Coal(peat inc)
- **Fuel:** Oil(common), Biofuel(unique)
- **Construction:** Stone(low), Plastic(org)
- **Minerals:** Sulfur(abundant), Uranium(swamp water)
- **Survival:** Food(std hunt, -40% farm), Oxygen-20% spores

## Unique Effect
Toxic spores dmg unprotected 30min

## Hostile Area
Overgrown alien station. Boss: Spore Queen Mother.
