# Alien Shield (Center Galaxy Reward) (SM-PR-07)

**Category:** Protection

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** Requires emitter module (2x2)
- **TIR:** Alien
- **Effect:** Energy-based barrier blocks ALL physical damage, projectile damage, and asteroid impacts
- **Energy Cost:** 100 energy/turn (while active). Shield strength scales with available energy reserves
- **Special:** When shield depletes (energy runs out), releases devastating pulse dealing 500 area damage to nearby enemies

## Notes
Makes ship invulnerable to physical/projectile damage while energy available. Vulnerable to energy-based attacks which bypass shield entirely
