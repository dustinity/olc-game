# Satellite Deployment Bay (SM-SC-04)

**Category:** Scanning

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 2x2
- **TIR:** 3
- **Range:** Planet-wide (when deployed)
- **Energy Cost:** 200 energy + satellite maintenance (10 energy/turn per active satellite)
- **Function:** Deploy up to 3 satellites for continuous planet monitoring
- **Capabilities:** Search for specific conditions over time, deliver data on map

## Notes
Essential for late-game exploration and base defense. Requires satellite bay module
