# Ion Storm Generator (SM-WP-10)

**Category:** Weapons

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 3x3
- **TIR:** 5
- **Damage:** Medium (area)
- **Fire Rate:** Pulse-based (3-second intervals)
- **Energy Cost:** 40 energy/turn while active

## Notes
Area denial. Affects friendlies and enemies. Strategic use required
