# Vision Research Category

Detection, scanning, targeting, and sensing technologies that expand awareness across planets and space. Vision research has no starting (Core) topic — all upgrades begin at Ring 1.

| Ring | Lab | Topics |
|------|-----|--------|
| Core | N/A | None — all vision unlocked through research |
| Ring 1 | Basic Forge | Basic Radar Array, Visual Enhancement I, Signal Processing I |
| Ring 2 | Energy Lab | Deep Sonar Array, Thermal Imaging, Laser Targeting System |
| Ring 3 | Dark Matter Lab | Satellite Deployment Bay, Tactical AI Assistant, Quantum Range Finder |
| Outer Ring | Void Lab | Quantum Scanner, Deep Void Sensors, Temporal Radar |

---

## Core -- Starting (No Research Required)

_No starting vision research -- all vision upgrades unlocked at Ring 1._

## Ring 1 -- Basic Forge Research

### Basic Radar Array

- **Time:** 5 minutes
- **Materials:** 200 construction material, 150 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** None (Ring 1 available at start)
- **Effect:** Short-medium radial scan range for planet and ship
- **Scan Range:** 200m on planet surface, 5km in space
- **Update Rate:** Once every 30 seconds (real-time tracking of scanned objects)
- **Size Required:** 1x1 grid slot on dropship, 2x2 on planet base
- **Energy Cost:** 10 energy/turn while active

### Visual Enhancement I

- **Time:** 2 minutes
- **Materials:** 50 minerals, 20 energy
- **Prerequisites:** None (Ring 1 available at start)
- **Effect:** +20% unit detection radius for all ground units
- **Applies To:** All infantry and vehicle units
- **Detection Radius Increase:** From base 30m to 36m per unit (or scaled equivalent for vehicles)
- **Ambush Detection:** +20% chance to detect hidden or camouflaged enemies before they detect you

### Signal Processing I

- **Time:** 3 minutes
- **Materials:** 100 construction material, 80 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Basic Radar Array complete
- **Effect:** Radar clutter reduction, -15% false positive rate for all scanning systems
- **Effective Range Increase:** Equivalent to +17% actual range due to cleaner signal (200m to effective 234m on planet)
- **Tracking Accuracy:** +10% lock-on speed for weapon targeting systems

## Ring 2 -- Energy Lab Research

### Deep Sonar Array

- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Materials:** 500 construction material, 400 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Basic Radar Array + Signal Processing I complete
- **Effect:** Underground structure detection for planet surface operations
- **Detection Depth:** Up to 50m below surface (reveals buried dungeons and structures)
- **Scan Range:** 500m radius from sonar array position
- **Update Rate:** Once every 60 seconds (slower than radar due to processing requirements)
- **Size Required:** 2x2 grid slot on planet base
- **Energy Cost:** 25 energy/turn while active

### Thermal Imaging

- **Time:** 8 minutes
- **Materials:** 300 construction material, 200 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Visual Enhancement I complete + Signal Processing I complete
- **Effect:** +100% detection range through terrain cover for all units
- **Detection Range Increase:** From 36m to 72m per unit (with Ring 1 enhancement factored in)
- **Terrain Penetration:** Sees through smoke, fog, light foliage, and dust storms
- **Heat Signature Display:** Thermal overlay on unit HUD shows enemy position through walls
- **Applies To:** All units with Vision upgrade slots

### Laser Targeting System

- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Materials:** 600 construction material, 500 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Deep Sonar Array + Thermal Imaging complete
- **Effect:** Precise single-target lock for weapons on ship and planet bases
- **Lock-On Time:** -50% (from base 2 seconds to 1 second for weapon acquisition)
- **Accuracy Bonus:** +25% hit probability for all locked targets
- **Range Extension:** Effective targeting range +40% over unassisted weapons
- **Size Required:** 1x1 grid slot on dropship, 2x1 on planet base
- **Energy Cost:** 15 energy/turn while active

## Ring 3 -- Dark Matter Lab Research

### Satellite Deployment Bay

- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Materials:** 800 construction material, 600 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Deep Sonar Array + Laser Targeting System complete
- **Effect:** Planet-wide monitoring with up to 3 satellites
- **Satellite Count:** Maximum 3 simultaneously deployed
- **Coverage per Satellite:** ~33% of planet surface each (99% total coverage with all 3)
- **Update Rate:** Continuous real-time monitoring of entire planet
- **Size Required:** 2x2 grid slot on dropship
- **Energy Cost:** 40 energy/turn for satellite constellation maintenance
- **Launch Time:** 5 minutes per satellite (cannot launch during combat)

### Tactical AI Assistant

- **Time:** 12 minutes
- **Materials:** 600 construction material, 400 minerals, 200 dark matter crystals
- **Prerequisites:** Thermal Imaging + Laser Targeting System complete
- **Effect:** Auto-target priority for highest threat, +150% detection range
- **Detection Range Increase:** From 72m to 180m per unit (with Rings 1 and 2 enhancements factored in)
- **Auto-Target Priority:** AI identifies and prioritizes highest-value targets automatically
- **Attack Speed Bonus:** +25% attack speed for AI-controlled units receiving targeting assistance
- **Applies To:** Heavy vehicles, mechs, aerial units, scout drones
- **Visual Change:** Unit gains small AI processor module with threat priority HUD indicators

### Quantum Range Finder

- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Materials:** 500 construction material, 300 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Satellite Deployment Bay + Tactical AI Assistant complete
- **Effect:** Instant distance calculation for all map features
- **Range Display:** Precise distance to every visible unit, structure, and resource deposit on HUD
- **Trajectory Calculation:** Automatic firing solution for all weapon types (accounts for range, wind, gravity)
- **Map Integration:** Displays exact coordinates for all discovered features
- **Size Required:** 1x1 grid slot on dropship
- **Energy Cost:** 10 energy/turn while active

---

## Outer Ring -- Void Lab Research

### Quantum Scanner

- **Time:** 20 minutes
- **Materials:** 2,000 construction material, 1,500 minerals
- **Prerequisites:** Satellite Deployment Bay + Quantum Range Finder complete
- **Effect:** Planet-wide instant map reveal
- **Reveal Scope:** Entire planet surface revealed simultaneously (no scanning time required)
- **Detail Level:** Full resolution -- all structures, resources, dungeons, and enemy positions visible immediately
- **Size Required:** 2x2 grid slot on dropship
- **Energy Cost:** 100 energy for single scan (one-time cost per planet visit)
- **Cooldown:** Can rescan any visited planet at any time for 50 energy

### Deep Void Sensors

- **Time:** 15 minutes
- **Materials:** 1,000 construction material, 800 dark matter crystals
- **Prerequisites:** Quantum Scanner + Satellite Deployment Bay complete
- **Effect:** Detect objects across light-years in deep space
- **Space Detection Range:** Unlimited (any object within visible space is detectable)
- **Planet Surface Enhancement:** +50% underground detection depth (from 50m to 75m with Deep Sonar Array)
- **Size Required:** 2x1 grid slot on dropship
- **Energy Cost:** 30 energy/turn while active in deep space, 10 energy/turn while in atmosphere

### Temporal Radar

- **Time:** 25 minutes
- **Materials:** 1,500 construction material, 1,200 dark matter crystals
- **Prerequisites:** Deep Void Sensors + Tactical AI Assistant complete
- **Effect:** Predict enemy movement patterns 3 seconds ahead
- **Prediction Window:** 3-second future movement projection for all visible enemies
- **Tactical Advantage:** +20% evasion rate when moving (enemies predict where you will be, you predict where they will be)
- **Combat Application:** Auto-calculated optimal firing solutions accounting for enemy predicted position
- **Size Required:** 3x2 grid slot on dropship
- **Energy Cost:** 50 energy/turn while active
