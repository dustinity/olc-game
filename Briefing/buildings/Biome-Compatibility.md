## Biome Details [See biomes](../Planets/Biomes/README.md)
- **Desert/Dusty:** Solar +30%, dust storms -40% radar
- **Rocky:** Geothermal +50%, rock formations +15% wall HP
- **Water/Swamp:** Water turbines (reduced in swamp), oil deposits
- **Jungle:** Coal/Biofuel, veg cover -15% move +20% defense
- **Light Snow/Ice:** Wind +25%/+40%, ice crystals abundant
