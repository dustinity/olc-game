# Energy Shield Generator (PB-SD-01)

**Tier:** Elite (TIR 4-5)
**Category:** Elite Production

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 2x2 (generator) + invisible field area
- **Cost:** 3000 construction material, 2000 minerals, 1000 energy (startup)
- **Power:** Requires 100 energy/turn (while active)
- **Function:** Creates area-wide protective dome (50m radius)
- **Effect:** All units inside dome receive +50% defense

## Notes
Massive energy drain. Can be toggled on/off strategically
