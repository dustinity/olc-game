# Orbital Strike Beacon (PB-SD-02)

**Tier:** Elite (TIR 4-5)
**Category:** Elite Production

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 2x2
- **Cost:** 2000 construction material, 1500 minerals, 800 energy (startup)
- **Power:** Requires 30 energy/turn + strike cost (50 energy per strike)
- **Function:** Calls down targeted bombardment on target area [See ship modules](../../../ShipModules/README.md)
- **Dependency:** Requires compatible mothership weaponry (rocket bays for missile strikes, energy cores for beam strikes)
- **Range:** Planet-wide if satellite link active [See navigation](../../../Planets/Navigation/README.md)
- **Cooldown:** 60 seconds between strikes
