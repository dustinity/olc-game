# Quantum Gate (PB-SD-03)

**Tier:** Elite (TIR 4-5)
**Category:** Elite Production

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 3x3 (each end)
- **Cost:** 4000 construction material, 3000 minerals, 2000 energy (startup per gate)
- **Power:** Requires 50 energy/turn per gate
- **Function:** Instant unit transport between two linked gates
- **Range:** Planet-wide. Base-to-base or base-to-ship transport

## Notes
Requires both gates to be active and linked. Strategic repositioning tool
