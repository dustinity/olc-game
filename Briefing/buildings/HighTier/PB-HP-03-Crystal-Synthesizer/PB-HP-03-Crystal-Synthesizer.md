# Crystal Synthesizer (PB-HP-03)

**Tier:** Elite (TIR 4-5)
**Category:** Elite Production

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 3x3
- **Cost:** 1500 construction material, 800 crystal minerals, 400 energy (startup)
- **Power:** Requires 30 energy/turn
- **Function:** Converts raw energy into crystal formations for advanced research
- **Output:** Generates dark matter crystals over time

## Notes
Essential for Void Lab fuel. Ice biome crystals provide +50% output
