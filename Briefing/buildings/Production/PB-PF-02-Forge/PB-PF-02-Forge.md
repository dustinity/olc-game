# Forge (PB-PF-02)

**Tier:** Advanced (TIR 2-3)
**Category:** Production Facilities

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 3x3
- **Cost:** 600 construction material, 400 minerals, 100 energy (startup)
- **Power:** Requires 25 energy/turn
- **Function:** Researches planet tech, military tech, building blueprints. Crafts unit upgrades [See upgrade system](../../../units/Upgrade-System/README.md)

## Notes
Planet-based research. Placing forge on planet unlocks planet tech tree [See tech tree](../../../Tech-Tree/Overview/README.md)
