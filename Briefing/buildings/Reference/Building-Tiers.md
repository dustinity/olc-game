# Building Tiers and Progression

## TIR Progression

| Tier | TIR Range | Building Complexity | Unlock Method |
|------|-----------|---------------------|---------------|
| Default | TIR 1-2 | Basic structures | Available at game start |
| Advanced | TIR 2-3 | Complex facilities | Forge research or dungeon blueprint |
| Elite | TIR 4-5 | Super structures | Void lab research or center galaxy loot |

## Tier Examples

### Default (TIR 1-2) — Available at Game Start
- **Extraction:** Mine, Oil Pump, Harvester Post
- **Power:** Solar Array, Wind Turbine, Coal Reactor, Water Turbine
- **Infrastructure:** Camp Barracks, Habitation Module, Radar Tower, Wall Segment, Gate
- **Storage:** Locker, Container, Haul Storage

### Advanced (TIR 2-3) — Forge Research or Dungeon Blueprint
- **Production:** Factory, Forge, Refinery, Workbench
- **Defense:** Turret Platform, Wall Gate (Advanced), Reinforced Wall
- **Support:** Med Bay, Command Center, Airfield

### Elite (TIR 4-5) — Void Lab Research or Center Galaxy Loot
- **Production:** Void Lab, Assembly Plant, Crystal Synthesizer
- **Defense:** Energy Shield Generator, Orbital Strike Beacon, Quantum Gate
- **Special:** Dungeon Scanner, Resource Converter, Alien Artifact Decoder
