# Dungeon Scanner (PB-SP-01)

**Tier:** Elite (TIR 4-5)
**Category:** Elite Production

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 1x1 (tall antenna)
- **Cost:** 1000 construction material, 800 minerals, 500 energy (startup)
- **Power:** Requires 25 energy/turn (continuous)
- **Function:** Periodically pulses to reveal nearby dungeons on map [See planet events](../../../Planets/Planet-Events/README.md)
- **Range:** Reveals dungeons within 5km radius
- **Cooldown:** Pulse every 10 minutes (in-game time)

## Notes
Essential for late-game exploration efficiency
