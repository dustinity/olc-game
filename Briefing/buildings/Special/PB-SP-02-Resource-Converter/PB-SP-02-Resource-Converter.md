# Resource Converter (PB-SP-02)

**Tier:** Elite (TIR 4-5)
**Category:** Elite Production

## Specs
- **Grid Size:** 3x3
- **Cost:** 1500 construction material, 1000 minerals, 600 energy (startup)
- **Power:** Requires 20 energy/turn
- **Function:** Converts any resource to any other at reduced efficiency (70% yield)

## Notes
Emergency fallback when specific resource is unavailable. Can convert excess into needed resources
