# Bright Realistic

**Aesthetic:** Clean civilian design, practical technology, optimistic future
**Playstyle:** Sustainable expansion, high survivability, efficient crafting

## Faction Bonuses

- **Survival consumption -40%:** Units consume 40% less food/oxygen
- **Crafting cost -30%:** All crafted items require 30% fewer materials
- **Unit HP regeneration +50%:** Soldiers regenerate health 50% faster in base
- **Hospital blueprint discovery +25%:** Higher chance of finding survivability blueprints

## Faction Units [See units](../../units/Unit-Types/README.md)

- **Field Medics:** Healing support units that revive and heal nearby soldiers
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 90 | Damage: 8 (shotgun for self-defense) | Range: 10m (healing), 15m (defense) | Speed: Medium | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Heals nearby units for 5 HP/sec. Bright Realistic exclusive at TIR 1, available to all from TIR 2.
- **Engineers:** Construction and repair specialists who build and maintain structures
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 100 | Damage: 10 (wrench throw) | Range: 5m (repair), 10m (defense) | Speed: Medium | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Repairs buildings at 20 HP/sec. Can construct emergency barricades. All factions.
- **Survival Scouts:** Self-sufficient scouts with extended operation time without resupply
  - TIR: 1 | HP: 80 | Damage: 12 (hunting rifle) | Range: 25m | Speed: Fast | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - -30% survival consumption while in field. Extended detection radius (+40%). Bright Realistic exclusive.

## Signature Building

**Hydroponics Bay** — Produces food and oxygen simultaneously. Output scales with TIR tier. Unlocked at faction start. Can be upgraded through tech tree.

## Ideal For

Players who enjoy sustainable resource management, long-term base building, and survival-focused gameplay.
