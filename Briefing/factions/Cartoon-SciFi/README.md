# Cartoon SciFi

**Aesthetic:** Exaggerated Borderlands-style tech, vibrant colors, over-the-top weapons
**Playstyle:** High mobility, explosive combat, fast space travel

## Faction Bonuses

- **Drive efficiency +40%:** All ship drives consume 40% less fuel
- **Weapon fire rate +30%:** All weapons shoot 30% faster (compensates for slightly lower per-shot damage)
- **Vehicle speed +25%:** All ground vehicles move 25% faster
- **Warp transit time -30%:** Alien wormhole passage is 30% shorter

## Faction Units [See units](../../units/Unit-Types/README.md)

- **Blaster Rangers:** Fast-moving soldiers with energy weapons
  - TIR: 1 | HP: 90 | Damage: 18 (energy pistol) | Range: 22m | Speed: Fast | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Higher fire rate compensates for lower per-shot damage. Cartoon SciFi exclusive at TIR 1, available to all from TIR 3.
- **Rocket Jockeys:** Vehicle-mounted rocket specialists
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 160 | Damage: 45 (rocket launcher) | Range: 50m | Speed: Fast | Grid: 1x1
  - Mounted on light vehicle chassis. Area damage from rocket explosions. Cartoon SciFi exclusive.
- **Jump Troopers:** Units with personal jump jets for rapid repositioning
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 120 | Damage: 20 (energy pistol) | Range: 15m | Speed: Very Fast | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Rapid repositioning via personal jump jets. Can cross terrain obstacles instantly. Cartoon SciFi exclusive at TIR 1, available to all from TIR 3.

## Signature Building

**Ion Thruster Pad** — Launches units forward with speed boost. Used for rapid deployment and tactical repositioning. Unlocked at faction start. Cannot be upgraded further.

## Ideal For

Players who enjoy fast-paced combat, space exploration, and exaggerated sci-fi action.
