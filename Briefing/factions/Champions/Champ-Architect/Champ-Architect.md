# Architect

**Role:** Morgan Lee

## Stats & Info
- **Dungeon runs:** +1 point per dungeon completed (unit must survive)
- **Boss kills:** +3 points per boss defeated (unit must deal the killing blow or be in engagement range)
- **Combat survival:** +0.5 points per 60 minutes of active combat time

## Abilities
- **Blueprint Vision (Lvl 5):** See optimal building placement layout for current resources
- **Efficiency Boost (Lvl 10):** One selected building operates at 200% efficiency for 30 seconds
- **Instant Structure (Lvl 20):** Construct one building instantly at target location (costs half normal materials)

## Passive
-20% all building costs, +15% building placement range
