# Gardener

**Role:** Elias Greenfield

## Abilities
- **Rapid Growth (Lvl 5):** Instantly mature one farm plot, yields 2x resources
- **Seed Bomb (Lvl 10):** Throw seed that grows into temporary plant covering 5m radius (slows enemies)
- **Bloom Zone (Lvl 20):** Creates 20m zone where food and oxygen production doubles for 60 seconds

## Passive
+30% food yield from all farming sources
