# Glitch

**Role:** Alex Chen

## Abilities
- **Cloak Field (Lvl 5):** Invisible for 15 seconds, movement breaks cloak after 3 seconds
- **Hologram Decoy (Lvl 10):** Creates holographic copy that draws enemy fire for 10 seconds
- **Data Siphon (Lvl 20):** Steal blueprint fragment from defeated boss (guaranteed TIR 1-2 blueprint)

## Passive
+25% exploration speed, reveals nearby dungeon silhouettes automatically
