# Ironclad

**Role:** Major Rachel Torres

## Abilities
- **Armor Up (Lvl 5):** +50% wall/turret HP for 60 seconds, cannot move while active
- **Battle Cry (Lvl 10):** Nearby units gain +20% damage and +10% armor for 30 seconds
- **Fortify Position (Lvl 20):** Instantly construct reinforced barricade at location (lasts 5 minutes)

## Passive
Units within base radius gain +15% HP from all sources
