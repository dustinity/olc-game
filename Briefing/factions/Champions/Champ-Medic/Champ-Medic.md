# Medic

**Role:** Dr. Amara Osei

## Abilities
- **Heal Pulse (Lvl 5):** Restore HP to all nearby units in 10m radius
- **Stim Shot (Lvl 10):** Instantly revive fallen ally with 30% HP (once per battle)
- **Field Hospital (Lvl 20):** Create temporary healing zone that restores 5 HP/sec for 30 seconds

## Passive
+25% unit HP regeneration rate in base radius
