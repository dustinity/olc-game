# Sniper

**Role:** Viktor Petrov

## Abilities
- **Scope Enhancement (Lvl 5):** Next shot pierces through all enemies in line for 10 seconds
- **Smoke Screen (Lvl 10):** Deploys smoke covering 15m radius for 20 seconds, units inside gain +30% defense
- **Precision Strike (Lvl 20):** Call orbital strike on target area (requires 10 second channel)

## Passive
+20% weapon range for all units with ranged weapons
