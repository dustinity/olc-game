# Voltage

**Role:** Kai Nakamura

## Abilities
- **Chain Lightning (Lvl 5):** Electric arc that jumps between 3 mechanical enemies, dealing escalating damage
- **Overclock (Lvl 10):** Temporarily doubles mine/refinery output for 60 seconds, then building cools down for 30 seconds
- **Neural Surge (Lvl 20):** Stun all electronic enemies in 10m radius for 5 seconds

## Passive
+10% mining speed when champion is present at mine site
