# Wrench

**Role:** Sam Okafor

## Abilities
- **Rapid Build (Lvl 5):** Place building in half the normal time, costs 10% more materials
- **Field Repair (Lvl 10):** Restore 50% of a building's HP instantly, once per minute per building
- **Modular Overhaul (Lvl 20):** Upgrade one building to next TIR tier for 5 minutes (temporary)

## Passive
-15% crafting costs for all players in base radius
