# Dark Realistic

**Aesthetic:** Gritty military sci-fi, armored soldiers, heavy weaponry
**Playstyle:** Defensive powerhouse, methodical base building, overwhelming firepower

## Faction Bonuses

- **Weapon damage +35%:** All soldier and turret weapons deal 35% more damage
- **Base defense HP +40%:** Walls, gates, and turrets have 40% more health
- **Unit training speed +25%:** Barracks produce soldiers faster
- **Turret range +20%:** All defensive turrets have extended range

## Faction Units [See units](../../units/Unit-Types/README.md)

- **Shock Troopers:** Heavily armored infantry with ballistic rifles
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 200 | Damage: 35 (heavy ballistic rifle) | Range: 30m | Speed: Medium | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Heavily armored frontline infantry. Dark Realistic exclusive at TIR 1, available to all from TIR 3.
- **Heavy Gunners:** Turret-mounted weapon specialists
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 150 | Damage: 25 (mounted turret) | Range: 40m | Speed: Slow | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Stationary or slow-moving heavy weapons platform. Can mount on walls and gates. All factions.
- **Armored Vehicles:** Tank units with reinforced plating and cannon fire
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 1,200 (+20% from faction bonus) | Damage: 60 (main cannon) | Range: 60m | Speed: Slow | Grid: 2x2
  - Standard tank chassis with Dark Realistic reinforced plating. Factory production.

| Tactical Comms | Morale Beacon, Command Relay | Buff aura for nearby units: +damage and +defense in radius |

## Signature Building

**Fortress Wall** — Reinforced wall segment with 3x normal HP. Can mount a turret slot on top. Unlocked at faction start. Cannot be upgraded further.

## Ideal For

Players who enjoy defensive base building, tactical combat, and military-themed gameplay.
