# Neon Punk

**Aesthetic:** Sleek mech suits, neon-lit circuits, cyberpunk urban decay
**Playstyle:** Aggressive resource extraction, fast early-game expansion, high-output mining operations

## Faction Bonuses

- **Mining output +50%:** All mine buildings produce 50% more resources
- **Refining efficiency +30%:** Refinery processing costs 30% less materials
- **Mine tool damage +40%:** Hand-mining yields significantly more resources in early game
- **Building placement speed +25%:** Structures placed faster, reducing downtime

## Faction Units [See units](../../units/Unit-Types/README.md)

- **Mech Workers:** Heavy exoskeleton units that mine and build simultaneously
  - TIR: 1 | HP: 180 | Damage: 12 (mining tool) | Speed: Slow | Grid: 1x1
  - Trained at Barracks. Dual-purpose: mines resources or constructs buildings. Neon Punk exclusive.
- **Neon Scouts:** Fast reconnaissance units with cloaking devices
  - TIR: 1 | HP: 60 | Damage: 8 (pistol) | Speed: Very Fast | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - Reconnaissance unit. Brief cloak ability (3 seconds, 30-second cooldown). Neon Punk exclusive.
- **Circuit Breakers:** Electric damage specialists, effective against mechanical enemies
  - TIR: 2 | HP: 130 | Damage: 30 (electric rifle) | Range: 25m | Speed: Medium | Grid: 0.5x0.5
  - +50% damage vs Mechanical and Crystalloid enemies. Neon Punk exclusive at TIR 1, available to all from TIR 3.

## Exclusive Equipment Categories [See equipment system](../../units/Equipment-System/README.md)

| Category | Examples | Effect |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Circuit Weapons | Shock Rifle, Arc Castor, "Overload" (Legendary) | Electric damage, +50% vs mechanical enemies |
| Cyber Implants | Reflex Booster, Quantum Refiner, Neural Link | Passive stat boosts while equipped |
| Cloaking Mesh | Stealth Vest, Phantom Plating | Short-duration stealth ability on demand |

## Signature Building

**Quantum Mine** — Produces 3x normal output but requires 2x energy. Unlocked at faction start. Cannot be upgraded further.

## Ideal For

Players who enjoy fast resource accumulation, aggressive early-game expansion, and mech-themed gameplay.
