# Crystal Shards

**Family:** Crystalloid (6 Sub-Races)

## Specs
- **TIR Range:** 1-2
- **Biome Preference:** Ice, Rocky (mineral-rich), Crystal Caves
- **HP:** Low | **Damage:** Medium | **Speed:** Slow
- **Behavior:** Fragile but multiply on death — each destroyed Shard splits into 3 smaller fragments
- **Special:** Sharp edges — any unit that melee attacks takes 2 damage in return
- **Weakness:** Sound/sonic weapons deal +100% damage
