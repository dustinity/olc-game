# Dark Matter Shapers

**Family:** Crystalloid (6 Sub-Races)

## Specs
- **TIR Range:** 4-5
- **Biome Preference:** Ice (poles), Crystal Caves, Center Galaxy
- **HP:** Very High | **Damage:** Extreme | **Speed:** Variable
- **Behavior:** Gravity manipulation elites that warp space around them
- **Special:** Gravity well — pulls all units within 15m toward center for 5 seconds, then releases devastating pulse
- **Weakness:** Light-based weapons disrupt gravity field, dealing +150% damage
- **Role:** Merchant NPCs found at abandoned stations or neutral outposts
- **Behavior:** Trade blueprints and resources for energy, minerals, and construction materials
- **Inventory:** Scales with player TIR — higher TIR = better items available
- **Special:** Will escort valuable cargo if protected from pirate attacks (reward: rare blueprints)
- **Role:** Non-hostold AI guardians of ancient structures
- **Behavior:** Stand motionless until approached, then communicate or test player
- **Interaction:** Give quests in exchange for specific resources or dungeon completion
- **Reward:** Unique blueprints, permanent stat boosts, key items for advanced areas
