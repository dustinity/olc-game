# Rustborn Clan

**Family:** Humanoid (7 Sub-Races)
**Disposition:** Always hostile

## Specs
- **TIR Range:** 2-3
- **Biome Preference:** Desert, Dusty, Rocky, Ruins, Industrial Wreckage
- **HP:** Medium | **Damage:** Medium-High | **Speed:** Medium
- **Behavior:** Ambush patrols, welded scrap fortifications, close-range pressure, intimidation markings
- **Special:** Blood Oath - nearby Rustborn gain +15% damage resistance after the first clan unit dies
- **Weakness:** Poor shields and weak long-range discipline; vulnerable to organized kiting and ion disruption

## Visual Signature
- Rusted gunmetal armor, welded scrap plates, red-stained hull markings, chipped helmets, exposed bolts
- Their ships and camps use brutal, patched construction rather than clean military layouts
- Should read as outlaw-humanoid and hostile immediately, without using readable text or symbols
