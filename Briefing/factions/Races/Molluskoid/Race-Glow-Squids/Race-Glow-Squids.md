# Glow Squids

**Family:** Molluskoid (6 Sub-Races)

## Specs
- **TIR Range:** 2-3
- **Biome Preference:** Water (deep ocean biomes)
- **HP:** Medium | **Damage:** Low-Medium | **Speed:** Medium
- **Behavior:** Bioluminescent blinding attacks that reduce enemy accuracy
- **Special:** Flash — all units in 10m radius miss next attack (3 second cooldown per unit)
- **Weakness:** Dark biomes reduce effectiveness, light-based weapons stun them
