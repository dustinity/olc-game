# Hydra Colonies

**Family:** Reptilian (6 Sub-Races)

## Specs
- **TIR Range:** 4-5
- **Biome Preference:** Water, Swamp
- **HP:** Very High | **Damage:** High | **Speed:** Medium
- **Behavior:** Multi-head regenerating units — each head is an independent attacker
- **Special:** Regeneration — loses a head at 66% and 33% HP, but regenerates 2 heads if main body survives 10 seconds
- **Weakness:** Focus fire to kill all heads before regeneration completes
