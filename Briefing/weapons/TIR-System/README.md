# TIR System - Technology/Threat Intensity Rating

5 TIR levels plus 1 Alien tier, with improvement cycles per tier.

---

## TIR Overview

TIR (Technology/Threat Intensity Rating) is the core progression scale in the game, ranging from 1 to 5 plus an Alien tier. It affects weapons, buildings, units, ship modules, and enemy races across all systems.

**Definition:** TIR represents both technological advancement and threat intensity — higher TIR means more powerful equipment on both player and enemy sides.

## Weapon TIR Levels

### Tier 1 (TIR 1) - Starting Technology

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Damage Multiplier | 80% of base |
| Range Multiplier | 80% of base |
| Unlock Method | Available at game start |
| Improvement Capacity | Up to level 3 (max +15% cumulative) |

**Available Weapons:**
- 2-Cannon Ballistic Turret
- Basic Energy Emitter (limited fire time)
- Small Rocket Bay (basic warheads only)
- Basic Ion Emitter (short duration)

**Notes:**
- Starting weapons on dropship
- Effective against TIR 1-2 enemies
- Limited ammo types
- Can be improved up to 3 times maximum

## Improvement Cycle System

### How Improvements Work

Each weapon can be improved up to 10 times through the improvement cycle system. Each improvement requires:
- Construction material (scales with TIR)
- Specific minerals (based on weapon category)
- Energy (for activation and calibration)
- Blueprint (unique to each improvement tier)

### Improvement Progression Table

| Improvement Level | Bonus Per Level | Cumulative Bonus | Total Weapon Damage | Total Range |
|-------------------|-----------------|------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Base (0) | — | 0% | TIR multiplier only | TIR multiplier only |
| 1 | +5% | +5% | ×1.05 | ×1.05 |
| 2 | +5% | +10% | ×1.10 | ×1.10 |
| 3 | +5% | +15% | ×1.15 | ×1.15 |
| 4 | +7% | +22% | ×1.22 | ×1.22 |
| 5 | +7% | +29% | ×1.29 | ×1.29 |
| 6 | +7% | +36% | ×1.36 | ×1.36 |
| 7 | +10% | +46% | ×1.46 | ×1.46 |
| 8 | +10% | +56% | ×1.56 | ×1.56 |
| 9 | +10% | +66% | ×1.66 | ×1.66 |
| 10 (Max) | +15% | +81% | ×1.81 | ×1.81 |

## TIR Progression Path

### Recommended Weapon Upgrade Order

1. **Early Game (TIR 1-2):** Start with ballistic turret → upgrade to 4-cannon → add small rocket bay
2. **Mid Game (TIR 2-3):** Add energy emitter for swarms → unlock torpedo launcher for heavy targets
3. **Late Game (TIR 3-5):** Deploy ion weapons for mechanical enemies → unlock void beam for ultimate damage
4. **End Game (Alien):** Gravity Well Emitter + alien void beam for complete battlefield control

### Blueprint Acquisition by TIR

| TIR | Primary Source | Secondary Source |
|-----|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | Small/medium dungeons | Starting dropship loot |
| 2 | Military camps, lost bunkers | Forge research (basic) |
| 3 | Hospital complexes, alien structures | Energy Lab research |
| 4 | Center galaxy outposts, boss rewards | Dark Matter Lab research |
| 5 | Inner ring fortress dungeons | Void Lab research |
| Alien | Final boss, central structure | Alien Artifact Decoder |
