# Ion Weapons

Electronic disruption weapons that disable mechanical and electronic targets. Minimal physical damage but devastating against drones, automatons, and technology-based enemies.

---

## Ion Emitter

- **TIR:** 3
- **Damage:** 10 physical + 50 electronic disruption
- **Fire Rate:** Continuous beam
- **Energy Cost:** 15 energy/turn while firing
- **Range:** 25m
- **Special:** Disables mechanical enemies for 5 seconds. Organic enemies receive -50% effectiveness (25% of normal)
- **Notes:** Essential vs drone swarms and mechanical enemies. Useless against purely organic foes

## Ion Storm Generator

- **TIR:** 5
- **Damage:** 30 physical + 100 electronic disruption (area pulse)
- **Fire Mode:** Pulse every 3 seconds
- **Energy Cost:** 40 energy/turn while active
- **Range:** 30m radius area
- **Special:** Creates expanding ion field disabling ALL electronics for 10 seconds (friendlies and enemies)
- **Notes:** Strategic area denial. Affects own units with electronic equipment — coordinate before use

---

## Ion Effectiveness Matrix

| Enemy Type | Physical Damage | Electronic Disruption | Duration |
|------------|-----------------|----------------------|----------|
| Mechanical (bugs, reptiles with tech) | 100% | 100% | 5-10 seconds |
| Drone/Automaton | 100% | 200% | 10-30 seconds |
| Organic (dinos, mollusks) | 100% | 50% | 2-5 seconds |
| Crystalloid | 100% | 75% | 3-8 seconds |
| Humanoid with armor | 100% | 150% | 5-15 seconds |
| Shielded units | 50% | 200% | 10+ seconds |
