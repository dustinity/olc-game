# Void Weapons

Spatial manipulation weapons that bypass conventional armor and shields. Ultimate damage output, dark matter fueled, effective against all target types.

---

## Void Beam

- **TIR:** 5
- **Damage:** 100/second (ignores all armor and shields)
- **Fire Mode:** Continuous
- **Energy Cost:** 40 energy/turn while firing
- **Range:** 30m
- **Special:** Direct HP damage — bypasses hull, shields, and armor completely
- **Notes:** Ultimate offensive weapon. Void Lab research required. Only dark matter crystals fuel ammo

## Gravity Well Emitter (Alien)

- **TIR:** Alien
- **Damage:** 500 total (area gravity implosion)
- **Fire Mode:** Pulse-based (pull for 5s, implode for 2s)
- **Energy Cost:** 60 energy/turn while active
- **Range:** 30m radius
- **Special:** Pulls all enemies toward center, then implodes dealing massive damage. Ignores shields and armor
- **Notes:** Ultimate area control. Alien tech required. Can pull friendly units too — position carefully

---

## Void Weapon Properties

- **Armor Ignore:** All void weapons deal 100% of base damage regardless of target armor
- **Shield Bypass:** Void damage passes through energy shields (but not Alien Shield) [See ship modules](../../../ShipModules/README.md)
- **Dark Matter Dependency:** Requires dark matter crystals as fuel source [See resources/types.md](../../../resources/README.md)
- **Spatial Distortion:** Visual effect warps space around weapon target — purple/black swirl with starlight edges
